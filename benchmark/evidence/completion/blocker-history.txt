The first completion attempt placed QLNativeChecks in the behavior aspect
model. That made mops make and the isolated Gradle build fail because the
generated Language.java referenced a missing
QuestionnaireLanguage.behavior.BehaviorAspectDescriptor.

The class was moved using mops to the language-local
QuestionnaireLanguage.checks model, and the behavior aspect was restored to
its empty state. The QuestionnaireLanguage module then made successfully;
the isolated Gradle language build later succeeded and compiled the helper.
The failed attempt is retained here rather than represented as a successful
build.
